Colorkin Support

Colorkin: Photo Coloring Book, for iPhone and iPad

Need help? Email ptteam.support@icloud.com. Tell us your device and iOS version, and what happened; a screenshot helps. We usually reply within two working days.

Making pages

Which photos work best?

Bright photos of people or pets, close up, with faces clearly visible. Two to four people work better than a big group. Very dark, blurry or far-away photos give rougher lines.

What is the difference between the styles?

  • Sketch is drawn on your device, is free and works offline.
  • Cartoon and Chibi are drawn by AI with cleaner lines. Each one uses one page from your plan or book, and needs an internet connection.

A page could not be drawn

Check your connection and try again. If it keeps failing, try another photo. A page that could not be drawn is not counted.

Books and printing

  • Make a book from the Home screen: choose a cover, add your pages, then open it to read, change layouts or arrange each page.
  • Save PDF makes a US Letter file ready to print at home or at a print shop. Print single-sided if you color with markers.
  • Video makes a short flip-through of the book to share.

Plans and purchases

What do the plans include?

  • Colorkin Pro (monthly or yearly): 60 AI pages a month and unlimited books. The yearly plan starts with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers, with 10 AI pages during the trial.
  • One Coloring Book: 30 AI pages and a printable book.
  • Three Coloring Books: 90 AI pages, three books' worth.

Any plan can make up to 20 AI pages a day.

Restore purchases on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app, tap the grown-ups button on the Home screen, then Restore purchases.

Cancel or change a subscription

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Colorkin Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Family Sharing

Colorkin Pro can be shared with up to five family members through Family Sharing. Each family member gets 20 AI pages a month. Book purchases cannot be shared.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your pages and books stay on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
