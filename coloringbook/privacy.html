Privacy Policy

Colorkin: Photo Coloring Book, for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 28 November 2026

Colorkin has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your pages, colors and books stay on your device. The one thing that leaves it is a photo you choose to turn into a cartoon or chibi coloring page, and our server does not keep that photo.

What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers is kept in its private storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Your coloring pages, the colors, stickers, words and frames you add, and your books
  • A copy of each photo you made a page from, so it can appear next to its page in a book
  • PDFs and videos you make, until you save or share them
  • Your settings and whether you have Colorkin Pro or a book purchase

None of this is sent to us. You can delete pages and books in the app; deleting the app removes everything it stored.

The app reads only the photos you pick with Apple's photo picker. It never asks for access to your whole photo library, your camera or your location.

Sketch pages

The Sketch style is drawn entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Cartoon and chibi pages

Before the first upload, the app asks for your permission. When you then ask for a cartoon or chibi page, the app sends this to our server:

  • The photo you chose, made smaller (about 768 × 1024 pixels) and with its hidden details, such as location, camera and date, removed
  • The style you picked
  • Apple's signed record of your purchase, so the server can confirm you have a plan or a book without an account

Our server passes the photo and a drawing instruction to Black Forest Labs, whose FLUX model draws the coloring page, and sends the page back to the app.

Our server does not store or log your photo or the page. Its logs record only whether each request succeeded. To keep count of the pages your plan or book includes, it keeps a counter under a one-way hash of your purchase's transaction number, which cannot be turned back into that number or linked to your Apple Account. The server runs on Cloudflare, which handles the network connection as part of delivering the request.

Black Forest Labs handles the photo under its own privacy policy. It states that it may keep the data it receives for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its service, and that it may use it to improve its models unless the customer opts out. We have asked Black Forest Labs to opt our account out of that use. Black Forest Labs Inc. is based in the United States; for people in the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, Black Forest Labs GmbH in Germany is responsible.

Please only use photos you have permission to use.

Purchases

Colorkin Pro and the coloring books are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details, billing address or Apple Account. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No account, login, email address or phone number
  • No sale or sharing of personal information for advertising

Children

Colorkin is made for parents and other adults, who choose the photos and make the pages; children may then color them. The app does not ask children for any personal information. The photos a parent chooses may show their family, including children, and are handled as described above.

Your rights (EEA, UK and elsewhere)

The photo is processed only because you ask for a page and agree to the upload; the purchase record is processed to provide what you bought. Because we keep no photos, pages, names or contact details, there is normally nothing of yours on our side to access or delete, beyond the anonymous page counter described above. You can still ask us about your data, ask us to delete anything we hold, or withdraw your permission at any time by not uploading further photos. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For requests about data held by Black Forest Labs, you can contact them at privacy@blackforestlabs.ai, or ask us and we will pass the request on.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or sends, or who draws the pages, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
