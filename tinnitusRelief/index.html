Notched - Support

Tinnitus masking for iPhone and Apple Watch

Something not working, or a question this page does not answer? Write to ptteam.support@icloud.com and you will get a reply from the person who built the app.

Getting started

Open Notched, pick a sound and press play. That is the whole app. Everything below is optional.

For the first seven days every feature is unlocked so you can find out whether masking helps you before paying for anything.

Common questions

How do I match the app to my own tinnitus pitch?

Tap the tuning fork button on the player, or open Settings and choose Tone Match. A pure tone plays; move the slider until it sounds like the ringing you hear, then save. Notched carves a notch out of the masking sound at that exact frequency, so the noise sits around your tinnitus rather than on top of it.

You can then switch the notch on for any sound, not just the built-in Notched Masking mix.

Does the Apple Watch app need my iPhone?

No. The Watch app generates its own sound. Leave the phone in another room, or at home. Audio plays through the Watch speaker, or through AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones paired to the Watch.

My mixes and my matched pitch are not on the Watch

The two apps sync over Apple's Watch Connectivity, which needs both apps to have been opened at least once and the Watch to be paired and nearby. Open the iPhone app, then open the Watch app, and give it a few seconds. If it still has not arrived, open Settings on either app and the sync runs again.

I bought Pro on my iPhone but the Watch still asks me to buy

Purchases follow your Apple Account, so you never pay twice. Open the Watch app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. If the Watch has been offline, bring it near your iPhone and open both apps once.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Settings, then Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

The sound stops when the screen turns off

On iPhone it should not - masking keeps playing in the background and appears on the Lock Screen. If it stops, check that Low Power Mode is off and that Notched has not been restricted in Settings, Battery.

On Apple Watch, keep the app in the foreground or start it from the complication; watchOS gives background audio less room than iOS does.

How do I stop it after I fall asleep?

Set a sleep timer on the player. The sound fades out rather than cutting off, and the fade length is adjustable in Settings.

What is free and what is Pro?

White noise and the sleep timer are free forever, with no trial and no purchase. Pro adds the full sound catalogue, layered mixes, haptic masking and Tone Match. It is sold either as a one-time purchase or as a monthly subscription - the same features either way.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Notched. Cancelling is handled entirely by Apple; the app cannot do it for you.

Does it use data, or need a connection?

No. Every sound is generated mathematically on the device as it plays. The app ships with no audio files and works in aeroplane mode. The only network request it ever makes is to Apple's own store, when you buy or restore.

A note on what this app is

Notched masks tinnitus with sound. Masking does not make tinnitus go away; it gives your hearing something else to settle on, which many people find makes the ringing easier to ignore, particularly at night. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. If your tinnitus is new, sudden, one-sided or accompanied by hearing loss or dizziness, see a doctor.
