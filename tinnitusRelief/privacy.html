Privacy Policy

Notched: Tinnitus Masking for iPhone and Apple Watch · Last updated 11 November 2026

Notched collects nothing about you. The app has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. It contains no networking code at all, so there is no mechanism by which your information could be sent anywhere.

What the app stores

Everything the app remembers is written to a private container on your own device and stays there:

  • Your settings - volume, fade lengths, sleep timer defaults, display options
  • Sound mixes you create
  • Your session history - when masking was played and for how long
  • The tinnitus pitch you matched in Tone Match
  • When your free trial began, and whether you have purchased Pro

None of this is transmitted. Deleting the app removes it.

Apple Watch

If you use both the iPhone app and the Apple Watch app, your mixes, matched pitch and purchase status are copied between the two using Apple's Watch Connectivity framework. That transfer happens directly between your own paired devices. It does not pass through us and we cannot see it.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details, billing address or Apple Account. The app only ever asks Apple a single question - whether this Apple Account is entitled to Pro - and stores the yes-or-no answer on the device.

Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No account, login, email address or phone number
  • No access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location or health data - the app never asks for a single system permission

Every sound is generated mathematically on the device as it plays. The app ships with no audio files and works with aeroplane mode on.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the app: ptteam.support@icloud.com
