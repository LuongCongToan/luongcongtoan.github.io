Haru Support

Haru: TOPIK Vocabulary, for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Questions, a wrong meaning, or something not working? Write to ptteam.support@icloud.com. Please include your device, the app version (Settings › Version) and, for a word, the word itself.

Subscriptions

  • Free trial: Haru Pro Yearly starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, one trial per Apple Account. You are not charged during the trial. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you will not be charged; you keep Pro until the trial ends.
  • Restore a purchase: open Settings in Haru and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Cancel or change plan: Settings in Haru › Manage subscription, or the Settings app on your iPhone › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid a renewal.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

  • No sound when a word is read. Haru uses the Korean voice built into iOS. If none is installed, go to Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › Korean and download one. Check that the ringer is not on silent.
  • No reminder arrives. Turn on Daily reminder in Haru's Settings, then allow notifications for Haru in the iPhone Settings app.
  • The sentence check does not answer. It needs an internet connection and Haru Pro. Each subscriber can check up to 20 sentences a day; the count starts again the next day.
  • Apple Watch shows Korean hints instead of meanings. Open Haru on your iPhone once with the Watch nearby; the meanings are sent from the iPhone.
  • Moving to a new iPhone. Your progress is stored on the device. Restore your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup to keep it.
