Privacy Policy

Haru: TOPIK Vocabulary, for iPhone and Apple Watch · Last updated 15 November 2026

Haru has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your study progress stays on your devices. The one thing that ever leaves them is a sentence you choose to have checked, and that sentence is not stored.

What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers is written to a private container on your own devices:

  • Your study progress: which words you have seen and when each one is due again
  • Your settings: interface and meaning language, daily new words, reminder time, speech speed
  • Whether you have Haru Pro

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

Apple Watch and widgets

If you use the Apple Watch app, your progress, settings and Pro status are copied between your iPhone and your Watch with Apple's Watch Connectivity framework. That transfer happens directly between your own paired devices and does not pass through us. The widgets read the same data from the app's shared container on the device.

Reminders and pronunciation

Study reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; there is no push server. Korean pronunciation uses the speech voice built into iOS and watchOS and runs on the device.

Sentence checking (Haru Pro)

When you write a Korean sentence and tap to have it checked, the app sends this to our server:

  • The sentence you wrote, and the word you were practising
  • The language the explanation should be written in
  • Apple's signed record of your subscription, so the server can confirm you have Pro without an account

The server passes the sentence, the word and the language to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the correction, and sends the result back to the app. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.

Our server does not store or log your sentence or the correction. Its logs record only whether each request succeeded. To keep to the limit of 20 checks a day, it keeps a counter under a one-way hash of your subscription's transaction number, which cannot be turned back into that number or linked to your Apple Account. The server runs on Cloudflare, which handles the network connection as part of delivering the request.

Sentence checking only happens when you ask for it. Everything else in the app works offline.

Purchases

Haru Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details, billing address or Apple Account. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No account, login, email address or phone number
  • No access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location or health data. The only permission it asks for is notifications, for study reminders

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
